Settings & Rules

How to Play

1. Arrange all 12 dice on the board.

2. Words must be 2+ letters unless 3-min is on. 3-min is classic Dabble.

3. All words must be connected in one cluster.

4. Green dice indicate a valid dictionary word.

5. Click on a green word for the definition.

6. Sound Effects may be enable/disabled.

7. NEW. Your level (0-10) will increase, +1, for each win and decrease with each loss. You will lose, -1, for each letter that is not green when the game ends; max -3

8. NEW. Share button shares the Dabble link including your letters for another player to play your letters.
